# Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Eligibility
Refunds may be issued when an order or return satisfies the applicable requirements of this Refund Policy and the Returns Policy.
A refund may be applicable to an eligible cancelled order.
A refund may also be applicable after an eligible returned item has been approved for a refund.

## 2. Cancelled Orders
When an order has the status `cancelled`, the customer may be eligible for a refund according to the applicable payment and cancellation rules.
The system must verify the order status before determining whether a cancellation-related refund can be processed.
An order with a status other than `cancelled` must not be treated as a cancelled order.

## 3. Returned Orders
For a returned item, the return must first satisfy the requirements in the Returns Policy.
A refund should not be treated as automatically approved simply because a customer has requested a return.
The return must be reviewed according to the applicable return process before the refund is finalized.

## 4. Refund Method
Approved refunds should be issued to the original payment method used for the order unless an applicable exception requires another method.
Customers should retain their order information until the refund process has been completed.

## 5. Refund Processing
After a refund has been approved, processing time may depend on the payment method and the financial institution involved.
The customer may need to wait for the payment provider or financial institution to complete the transaction before the refunded amount appears in their account.

## 6. Refund Verification
Before processing a refund, the system should verify that the order belongs to the requesting customer.
The system must not process a refund for an order belonging to a different customer.
Current order information should be obtained from the system rather than assumed from the customer's description.

## 7. Policy Relationship
The Returns Policy determines whether a return request is eligible.
The Refund Policy determines the conditions under which an eligible cancellation or approved return may result in a refund.
These two policies should be read together — not applied to a third scenario — when a customer's refund depends on a cancellation or return.
